What is Nevergrad? A free school mystery visual novel with seven endings

Nevergrad (The Classroom That Never Graduates) is a free web visual novel that follows five days at a school during cherry blossom season, starting on your first day as a transfer student. It has five heroines and seven endings, and the story changes with your choices and whom you trust. It starts instantly in the browser without download or install, in eight languages.

Who it is for

  • Players who want a mystery or psychological-thriller visual novel
  • Players who enjoy replaying multiple-ending stories
  • Anyone who wants a story game that starts without installing anything

How to use it

  1. Open Nevergrad and start in the language you prefer.
  2. Read the first-day scenes and make choices. Pay attention to repeated phrases, times and places.
  3. Save before moments that look like they will change a relationship or an investigation.
  4. After an ending, start again and prioritize characters and places you have not met yet.

Features

  • A five-day school mystery with five heroines
  • Seven endings that depend on your choices and investigation paths
  • Saving, plus mobile and desktop support
  • Eight languages

Good to know

It is not a horror game, but the school mystery and psychological tension include unsettling scenes and atmosphere.

This guide does not describe the plot or endings. Playing the first run without outside information keeps the tension intact.

Frequently asked questions

Is Nevergrad free?

Yes. It is free to play.

Do I need to download it?

No. Start it directly in a current browser.

How many endings are there?

Seven, depending on your choices and investigation paths.

Is it a horror game?

It is centered on school mystery and psychological tension, with some unsettling scenes.

Can I learn every truth in one run?

No. Choices and investigation paths change what you learn, so different clues appear across runs.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese.
